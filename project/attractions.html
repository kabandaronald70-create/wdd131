Uganda’s Must‑See Attractions

From the equator line to misty waterfalls — every corner tells a wild story.

Featured Attractions

Equator

Kayabwe, Central Uganda

The Uganda Equator is a famous tourist attraction where you can stand in both hemispheres. Watch water experiments, take photos, and buy unique souvenirs.

Wildlife Safari

National Parks across Uganda

Uganda is home to gorillas, lions, elephants, giraffes, and over 1,000 bird species. Visit Bwindi, Murchison Falls, or Queen Elizabeth NP for unforgettable encounters.

Kasubi Tombs

Kampala, Uganda

A UNESCO World Heritage site, the Kasubi Tombs are the royal burial grounds of the Buganda kings. This sacred site showcases traditional Ganda architecture made of wood, thatch, and reeds, and is a living cultural treasure.

Bwindi Mountain Gorillas

Southwestern Uganda

Home to half of the world's mountain gorillas. A once‑in‑a‑lifetime trekking experience deep in the ancient jungle.
