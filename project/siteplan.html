Site Name: Uganda Untamed

This name captures the raw, wild beauty of Uganda’s national parks, waterfalls, and wildlife. It suggests adventure and authenticity, appealing to travelers seeking off-the-beaten-path experiences.

Site Purpose

The website serves as a tourism guide to Uganda’s top attractions, including Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, Murchison Falls, Lake Bunyonyi, and the Kasubi Tombs. It provides destination information, image galleries, travel tips, and activity guides such as gorilla trekking, bird watching, and cultural tours. The site aims to promote Uganda’s natural and cultural heritage while helping travelers plan their visits.

Scenarios

  • What are the best national parks in Uganda to see mountain gorillas and how do I book a trekking permit?
  • How can I plan a trip that includes Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, Murchison Falls, Lake Bunyonyi, and the Kasubi Tombs?
  • What activities can visitors enjoy at Uganda’s national parks and cultural heritage sites?
  • When is the best time to visit Uganda for gorilla trekking, wildlife safaris, and bird watching?
  • What travel tips and requirements should international visitors know before traveling to Uganda?

The primary dark teal-green reflects Uganda’s lush forests. The warm beige background provides a natural, earthy feel. The orange accent adds energy and calls to action.

Typography

Montserrat (Headings) – Bold, clean, modern
Lato (Body text) – Readable, friendly, open

Headings (h1-h3) use Montserrat with 600/700 weight. Body text and paragraphs use Lato at 400/700. This combination ensures strong visual hierarchy and excellent readability on screens.
